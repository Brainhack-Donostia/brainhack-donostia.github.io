BHD2026 Projects

Meet the first confirmed projects for BrainHack Donostia 2026.

Beyond Speech

Sonia Zaragoza Baena · BCBL

This project investigates whether full Sign Language immersion led by native Deaf or CODA signers can support communication and reshape language-related brain activity in non-oral and minimally verbal people. The team will develop an open, reproducible fNIRS/EEG workflow using MNE-Python, BIDS, DataLad and Jupyter.

Reproducibility checks for journals, run on GitHub

Pandelis Perakakis · Complutense University of Madrid

This hands-on project explores a GitHub-based workflow for organising reproducibility checks for academic journals. Participants will consider how transparent, collaborative and reusable checks can strengthen open scholarly publishing.

RNN Night Fever: Does hippocampal replay emerge when recurrent neural networks go to sleep?

Alejandro Tabas · BCBL

Can recurrent neural networks spontaneously replay past experiences during simulated sleep to consolidate memory? This project brings together three interacting networks representing perception, the hippocampus and the medial prefrontal cortex. By alternating “day” and “night” phases, we will investigate whether replay emerges as a useful learning strategy rather than being explicitly programmed. Participants will help implement the networks, design their training and analyse their activity, with the aim of developing a submission to CCN2027.
