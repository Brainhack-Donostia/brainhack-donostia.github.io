Beyond Speech

Sonia Zaragoza Baena · BCBL

This project investigates whether full Sign Language immersion led by native Deaf or CODA signers can support communication and reshape language-related brain activity in non-oral and minimally verbal people. The team will develop an open, reproducible fNIRS/EEG workflow using MNE-Python, BIDS, DataLad and Jupyter.

Reproducibility checks for journals, run on GitHub

Pandelis Perakakis · Complutense University of Madrid

This hands-on project explores a GitHub-based workflow for organising reproducibility checks for academic journals. Participants will consider how transparent, collaborative and reusable checks can strengthen open scholarly publishing.

RNN Night Fever: Does hippocampal replay emerge when recurrent neural networks go to sleep?

Alejandro Tabas · BCBL

Replay is the reactivation of past experience during rest and sleep. It is thought to transfer knowledge from the hippocampus to the neocortex, but most models build replay in by hand rather than asking why it should arise.

In this project we will build a system of three recurrent neural networks (one representing perceptual processing, one representing a fast-learning hippocampal network, and one representing a slow-learning medial prefrontal cortical network), and study whether replay emerges as an optimal strategy for memory consolidation.

We will train the circuit on a perceptual task (to denoise some noisy sensory inputs) that requires the internal representation of a generative model of the sensory input. We will use day (during which the perceptual network needs to denoise the data with the help of the other two networks) and night (during which the networks will be free to do as they please) epochs and let the system meta-learn the best learning strategy. After learning, we will study whether replay emerged during the night epochs as a learning strategy.

Participants will help implement the networks, design the training curriculum, and develop analyses that detect and characterise replay in network activity. We aim to turn the results into a submission for CCN2027 (ccneuro.org/).
