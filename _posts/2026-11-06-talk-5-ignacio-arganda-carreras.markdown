description: "Deep learning has transformed bioimage analysis, but its adoption is still limited by the technical barriers that most life-science researchers face. BiaPy is an open-source Python library that makes deep learning for bioimage analysis accessible to everyone, with an intuitive graphical interface, ready-to-use training workflows, and reproducible models for segmentation, denoising, super-resolution, classification, and self-supervised learning. This talk introduces BiaPy's design and main features, and shows how it lowers the barrier to applying deep learning to microscopy images while promoting open and reproducible science.<br /><br />
<strong> About </strong><br />
Ignacio Arganda Carreras is an Ikerbasque Associate Research Professor at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), affiliated with the Biofisika Institute (CSIC-UPV/EHU) and the Donostia International Physics Center (DIPC). His research focuses on bioimage analysis and machine learning for microscopy. He is the lead developer of BiaPy and a core contributor to the ImageJ/Fiji ecosystem, including the Trainable Weka Segmentation plugin, working to make open-source, reproducible image-analysis tools available to the life sciences.<br /><br />
