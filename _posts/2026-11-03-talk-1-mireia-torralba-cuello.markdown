<strong> About </strong><br />
Mireia Torralba Cuello is a Serra Húnter Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya. Her research interests include the neural correlates of human cognition, neuroimaging and open science practices. Before joining UPC in 2025, she was a postdoctoral researcher at the Center for Brain and Cognition at Universitat Pompeu Fabra.<br /><br />
